# Cirkles name vocabulary

`lib/botswanaNames.ts` is a curated, deduplicated vocabulary covering Setswana/Botswana names, regional given names, Southern African surnames, English given names and nickname variants. It is not a frequency ranking, identity database, exhaustive language dictionary or evidence of anyone's ethnicity/gender. No student profile roster or full named-person record is scraped or shipped.

Research checks include Statistics South Africa's [2024 naming overview](https://www.statssa.gov.za/?p=19000), [2020 recorded births](https://www.statssa.gov.za/publications/P0305/P03052020.pdf), and [2017 naming discussion](https://www.statssa.gov.za/?p=11472). These support regional names including Lethabo, Omphile, Lesedi, Melokuhle, Lubanzi and Onalerona, English-word name collisions, and surnames including Dlamini, Nkosi, Ndlovu and Mokoena. Botswana Government DailyNews [local reporting](https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/46718) supports locally used given names/surnames. These sources validate representative patterns; they do not establish campus-wide frequency for every manually curated entry. The requested vocabulary is also included; nickname variants are explicitly labelled as editorial, not official name statistics.

Keep ordinary-word collisions context-sensitive. Exact high-confidence names are blocked; fuzzy matching is limited to longer regional names and excludes everyday vocabulary. A failed automated example is a regression to investigate, not a reason to pretend a dictionary can understand all stories. Test both bypasses and benign Setswana/English text when changing the list. Common places/institution names are not person identities by themselves. Safe academic/housing questions must remain usable.

Gemini's [structured output documentation](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/structured-output) and [Models API](https://ai.google.dev/api/models) inform the separate bounded semantic classifier. Provider output and student text are untrusted. A provider outage falls back to deterministic checks and is recorded as a mode, never presented as successful AI review.
