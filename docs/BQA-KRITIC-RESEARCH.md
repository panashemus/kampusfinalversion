# Botswana academic grounding, research and catalogue (8 November 2026)

## Source hierarchy
KAMPUS Kritic is an independent academic feedback tool, not an accredited assessor. BQA quality-assures qualifications and learning programmes, and sets assessment-policy principles, not a universal lecturer marking rubric. Priority: (1) actual assignment brief and rubric, (2) directly relevant institution rules if verified, (3) BQA general outcomes-based assessment and feedback guidance, (4) general academic best practice explicitly labelled as such.

## Scope and limitations of course expansion
The KAMPUS authentication/selection system currently supports five groups (UB, BAC/BSBS, BIUST, Botho, BUAN). BQA provider-specific NCQF Level 7 and 8 degree names from the public programme directory (pages 12–18) have been added for these five alongside the existing catalogue. These are source-listed titles, NOT proof of a programme's current accredited status, nor an exhaustive export covering all Botswana providers. Registration of a qualification and accreditation of an institution's learning programme are different. BQA's published register is dynamic, paginated and may change.

Students may now self-report a missing programme name rather than being locked out. Self-reported names are never advertised as BQA-accredited.

To fulfill a verified, *exhaustive* national inventory, KAMPUS should obtain BQA's current structured provider-programme export including programme IDs, ETP IDs, level, approval status, effective dates, expiry and last-reviewed dates; import with provenance and periodic reconciliation. Do not guess email eligibility for other providers. No institution-specific marking rubrics or private student distinction papers have been imported.

## Operational limits
Kritic uses server-side authentication, quotas and a configured Gemini model. It cannot guarantee marks, fabricate lecturer expectations, invent citations or imply BQA endorsement. Existing payments stay paused. Never place private coursework in public storage or use it as a contributed exemplar without consent and moderation.
